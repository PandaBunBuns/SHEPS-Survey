SHEPS Loading Survey

WELCOME

Press Start when instructed by your facilitator.

INSTRUCTIONS

There will be 2 Parts to this survey.

Please press 'F11' to enter full-screen mode.

Please keep your eyes on the screen.

Please refrain from using your phone or other devices during the survey.

PART 1

You will be shown 6 different loading screens.

They will last anywhere from 1-20 seconds.

Each loading screen's duration is randomized.

After each screen, there will be a short survey on your experience.

After completing the questions, you will proceed to the next screen.

Please refrain from asking the facilitator any questions while observing the loading screens.

If you have any questions, please ask now.
